Escape From Cube Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

Controller

The data controller is Paweł Trela, a sole proprietor based in Poland. For any privacy matter, contact: pawel.trela.dev@gmail.com.

What we don't collect

The game needs no account or sign-in. We run no servers of our own and do not collect your name, email address or any other data that would let us identify you.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, coins, cosmetics, settings and purchase records are stored only on your device. We never receive them. You can delete them by deleting the game or choosing “Reset progress” in the game settings (real-money purchases can always be restored).

In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We have no access to your card or Apple Account details. The game only receives a purchase confirmation (a transaction identifier) from Apple and stores it on your device to grant the items you bought and to avoid paying out a bonus twice. Apple's policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows Google AdMob ads: full-screen ads between levels and rewarded ads you choose to watch. To serve ads, Google and its partners may process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) – only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt,
  • your IP address and approximate location, device and system information,
  • ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic data.

Personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we show Google's consent form on first launch. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy settings. You can change tracking permission in iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Without consent, ads may still appear but are not tailored to you.

Google may transfer data outside the European Economic Area under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. More: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

The “No Ads” purchase removes full-screen ads. Rewarded ads remain, but only you can start them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Write to us: pawel.trela.dev@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (uodo.gov.pl) or your local authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
